Format: 1920×1080, 25 fps, 30 s. Colours: near-black #0B1220, electric blue #19B5FE, steel grey, white. Font: one bold sans (e.g. Montserrat ExtraBold + Regular).
Music: one cinematic/electronic track with a clear beat (YouTube Audio Library). Cut on the beats. Optional: subtle whoosh SFX on transitions.
